## 1. LEAD — HOUTHIS HIT RIYADH'S PIF TOWER — 11 WOUNDED IN SECOND STRIKE IN A WEEK

Yemen's Houthis struck the 80-storey, 385-metre Public Investment Fund Tower in Riyadh's
King Abdullah Financial District on Tuesday 24 November, wounding 11 people — 2 Saudis,
2 Indians, 3 Nepalis and 4 Egyptians, per Saudi Civil Defense. It is the second hit on the
same tower in under a week: the first, on 19 November, damaged the tower but hurt no one.
The Saudi-led coalition said it was a drone attack and vowed a "decisive" response.

Sources: Reuters, Bloomberg, AFP.

## 2. 2 DEAD, 40 WOUNDED AS RUSSIAN STRIKE RIPS INTO ZAPORIZHZHIA FLATS

An overnight Russian attack partially destroyed a five-storey residential building in the
Kosmichnyi district of Zaporizhzhia and destroyed a private house. Governor Mykhailo
Semikin, on Tuesday 24 November: 2 killed, 40 wounded, including 4 children — the youngest
an infant of a few months.

Sources: Reuters, The Kyiv Independent.

## 3. ISRAEL KILLS HAMAS INTERNAL SECURITY CHIEF IN GAZA CITY STRIKE

An Israeli airstrike killed Naeem Abu Nahel, 50, director of Hamas' internal security
apparatus in Gaza, in western Gaza City on Tuesday 24 November. He had held the post only
since September. The IDF and Shin Bet confirmed striking a senior Hamas militant; Prime
Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and Defence Minister Israel Katz announced him "eliminated".
At least 7 Palestinians were killed in Gaza on Tuesday, including 2 police officers.

Sources: Reuters, BBC News.

## 4. STATES MAY TURN BACK MIGRANTS IN 'HYBRID WAR' EMERGENCIES, TOP EUROPE COURT RULES

The European Court of Human Rights Grand Chamber ruled on Tuesday 24 November that states
may push back migrants when migration is "instrumentalised" by a hostile state, without
automatically breaching the convention — vulnerable people to be screened first. Three
judgments concerned Poland, Latvia and Lithuania over 2021–22 pushbacks to Belarus
(migrants from Afghanistan, Iraq and Cuba). Violations were found only in specific cases;
in the Lithuania case there was no violation, by 11 votes to 6. Amnesty International
called the rulings "a devastating blow to the absolute prohibition of torture".

Sources: ECHR official press release, Amnesty International, Arab News.

## 5. BUS SLAMS INTO PARKED TRUCK IN RAS AL KHAIMAH — 13 KILLED, 14 HURT

A passenger bus carrying 41 people hit a truck parked on Al Watan Road, Ras Al Khaimah, in
the early hours of Tuesday 24 November. 11 people were killed at the scene and 2 more died
in hospital — 13 dead, 14 injured — Ras Al Khaimah Police confirmed. The Indian consulate
in Dubai confirmed several Indian nationals among the dead. It is the UAE's second deadly
bus crash in under two weeks (14 killed in Fujairah on 12 November).

Sources: Reuters, Gulf Business, Khaleej Times.

## 6. CAR BOMB AT COLOMBIA GOVERNOR'S OFFICE WOUNDS 20+ ON EVE OF PEACE ANNIVERSARY

A car bomb detonated outside the Meta department governor's office in Villavicencio,
central Colombia, on Monday night, injuring 20+ people (reports of 22–24) and damaging
80+ buildings; no deaths. The car carried 80–100 kg of pentolite. President Abelardo de la
Espriella visited on Tuesday 24 November and blamed ex-FARC dissidents of the EMC's 39th
Front, naming commander Antonio Medina.

**Dating note:** the blast itself was late Monday 23 November, but it was first majorly
reported on Tuesday 24 November — the competition counts events that happened OR were
first reported on the day — and the presidential visit, the attribution to the EMC's 39th
Front, and the casualty/building-damage confirmations all landed on Tuesday, the eve of
the 10th anniversary of Colombia's 2016 peace deal.

Sources: Al Jazeera, Colombia Reports.
